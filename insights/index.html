Insights

Practical legal perspectives.

Short guides designed to help clients recognise the legal questions that deserve attention before a decision, transaction or dispute moves too far.

Succession

Preparing for succession: what information should a family gather first?

Start with the death certificate, family structure, known assets and liabilities, land records, bank information and any existing will or estate document.

Property

Before buying property, verify more than the title.

Ownership, searches, encumbrances, rates, approvals, boundaries and the terms of the sale agreement all deserve proper review.

Employment

Why written employment terms matter when a dispute begins.

Clear contracts, policies, notices and records can define rights and obligations long before a matter reaches formal proceedings.

Commercial

A contract should explain what happens when things go wrong.

Payment, performance, termination, liability and dispute clauses often become the most important parts of an agreement once a relationship is under strain.

Recovery

Debt recovery works best when documents are organised early.

Invoices, agreements, correspondence, acknowledgements and payment records can shape both negotiation and enforcement strategy.

Disputes

Not every dispute should begin in court.

Negotiation, structured settlement and other forms of dispute resolution can sometimes preserve time, cost and commercial relationships.
