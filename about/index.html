About the firm

A practical legal practice serving Nakuru and beyond.

Abuya M. & Associates Advocates advises and represents businesses, families and individuals across commercial, property, employment, litigation, succession and private-client matters.

Legal advice built around clarity, preparation and service.

The firm's work begins with understanding the client's objective, the legal position and the risks that may affect the outcome. From there, the focus is on a clear strategy, proper documentation and disciplined follow-through.

Its practice spans corporate and commercial work, litigation and dispute resolution, property and conveyancing, family and succession, employment matters, debt recovery and notarial services.
